# Sentyr edit guide — all prose and names are draft

This pack is an offered play-space, not a request that you bless our lore. Its
mechanical skeleton is already shaped to fit the engine; every story assertion
is yours to keep, rename, contradict, seal, or delete. Nothing in `pack.json`
becomes alpha canon through play, popularity, a token vote, or our implementation.

## The proposed slice

The recurring place is a patched human galley beside expedition return lifts.
Players can take one of several daily servings, receive an equally real
alternative when a featured serving is gone, perform a seven-step maintenance
task, inspect old field records, and provision a crew. The first expedition
follows a repaired return line into three possible routes: a maintenance spine,
a signal gallery, or a sealed nave. Four exact roles get different private
facts. Two specialist recommendations can authorize a safe return; going deeper
requires all four people to agree.

There are eight encounters, but only a few mechanical facts: a clearance marker
moves under observation; a truss responds before load; a pressure sequence opens
a phase edge; a finite signal crosses four instruments; visible points move
around carried objects; a two-role observation exposes a seam; a cradle performs
an all-or-nothing equal-mass exchange; and a control-less lift arrives at a
committed window. The pack intentionally does **not** answer what any of that
means.

## Safe to edit freely

- Every working name, sensory line, serving, maintenance prompt, archive hint,
  relic shape, and consequence description.
- The number and arrangement of rooms, as long as an extraction path remains.
- Which route each specialist privately recommends.
- Which encounters are shared or route-specific.
- Whether any deep route yields a relic at all.
- Every promotion hook. Deleting all of them leaves a complete beta game.

## Mechanical constraints to preserve or deliberately redesign

- Four roles are currently `pathfinder`, `engineer`, `containment`, and
  `quartermaster`, matching the crew mission kernel. A different roster is an
  engine change, not merely content editing.
- Safe extraction is supported by two specialist route recommendations. Deep
  recovery is full-crew unanimous. This is meant to make “go home” easier than
  “risk everyone for the thing.”
- Each route has both a return-now and descend-further outcome, exact operational
  cost, bounded contribution, and explicit recovery consequence.
- The graph is directed and extraction-reachable. Wrap/mirror tags are presently
  presentation metadata; finite phase transitions are semantic.
- The maintenance procedure is ordered and capped at eight actions.
- A daily choice that reaches capacity has authored alternative copy; a late
  visitor is never treated as a failed player.
- A discovered relic is not automatically an owned, tradable, or Bazaar-eligible
  object. This draft marks every candidate non-market-eligible.

## Canon controls

Mechanical truth means “the activated game and its instruments behave this
way,” not “the series has established this explanation.” Every artifact and
relic has `interpretation: null`. Each encounter lists its unresolved questions.
Promotion hooks have `alphaValue: null`; only your later explicit curator action
can fill an alpha successor. If you decline a hook, old beta receipts remain
honest records of the game without obligating the show.

Before activation we should replace working IDs with content-addressed artifact
references, compile rules into the Lean-owned mission surfaces, preview every
visible fact and contribution, sign a new content epoch, and run the release
gate. Until that ceremony, this directory is only a private writing room.
